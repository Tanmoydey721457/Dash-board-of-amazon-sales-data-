# 📊 Amazon Sales Data Dashboard

## 📝 Project Description
This interactive Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of **Amazon sales performance** across multiple dimensions such as shipping modes, countries, cities, categories, and markets.  
It is designed to help the **business team** quickly identify trends, profitability drivers, and operational bottlenecks.

---

## 🚀 Key Features
- **KPIs (Cards):**
  - Total Quantity: **7,102**
  - Total Sales: **266.7K**
  - Total Profit: **28.48K**
  - Total Shipping Cost: **31.10K**

- **Sales by Ship Mode (Line Chart):**
  - Standard Class dominates sales volume.
  - Same Day shipping contributes the least, highlighting potential for premium delivery strategy.

- **Sales by Country (Bar Chart):**
  - Top contributors: **Egypt (31.17K)**, **Morocco (30.78K)**, **South Africa (27.73K)**.
  - Nigeria lags behind at **18.01K**, suggesting untapped market potential.

- **Sales by City (Bar Chart):**
  - Strong performance in **Kinshasa, Casablanca, Alexandria, Johannesburg**.
  - Cities like **Lagos** and **Dar es Salaam** show moderate sales, indicating room for growth.

- **Sales by Category (Donut Chart):**
  - **Office Supplies** account for 100% of sales in this dataset.
  - Future expansion into **Furniture** and **Technology** categories could diversify revenue streams.

- **Sales by Market (Donut Chart):**
  - Entire dataset represents the **African market**.
  - Opportunity to expand analysis to **APAC, Canada, and other regions**.

---

## 💡 Business Insights
- **Profitability:** Despite high shipping costs (31.10K), overall profit remains strong (28.48K). Optimizing logistics could further improve margins.
- **Regional Focus:** Egypt, Morocco, and South Africa are the top-performing countries — ideal for targeted marketing campaigns.
- **City-Level Strategy:** Kinshasa and Casablanca are high-value cities; focusing on these hubs can maximize ROI.
- **Category Limitation:** Current dataset is restricted to Office Supplies. Diversification into other categories is essential for sustainable growth.
- **Shipping Mode Optimization:** Standard Class is dominant, but exploring faster delivery options could attract premium customers.

## 📂 How to Use
1. Clone this repository.
2. Open the `.pbix` file in **Power BI Desktop**.
3. Interact with filters (Category, Market) to explore different views.
4. Use insights to guide **business decisions** and **market strategies**.
